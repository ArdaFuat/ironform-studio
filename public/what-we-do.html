Process

A production workflow made for sharp geometry.

From model selection to final coating, every decision is made to keep the sculpture clean, strong and visually striking.

The process begins with choosing or developing the model. The form is modeled and translated into buildable drawings. Once the drawing stage is complete, steel parts are laser-cut, shaped into the correct angles, welded, refined and finished for indoor or outdoor use.

1
Model selection & sculptingA low-poly animal, emblem or object is selected, refined and prepared as a sculptural 3D form.
2
Build drawingsThe 3D form is translated into clean build drawings with panels, angles and assembly logic.
3
Laser cutting & formingSteel sheets are laser-cut, then each piece is bent and shaped into its proper low-poly angle.
4
Welding & finishThe sculpture is welded, refined, cleaned and finished with the right coating for its space.

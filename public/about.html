The Studio

An idea in metal.
A signature in space.

Ironform Studio creates geometric metal sculptures for homes, workspaces and architectural interiors. Animal forms and sharp planes give each piece its character.

From the silhouette to the reflected light, we care about how a piece lives in a space. Explore the collection or talk to us about a new design.

Process

From sharp model to finished steel.

01

Model selection & sculpting

A low-poly animal, emblem or object is selected, refined and prepared as a sculptural 3D form.

02

Laser cutting & forming

Steel sheets are laser-cut, then each piece is bent and shaped into its proper low-poly angle.

03

Welding & finish

The sculpture is welded, refined, cleaned and finished with the right coating for its space.

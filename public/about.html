About Ironform

Steel, with a point of view.

From animal sculptures and geometric wall art to freestanding statement pieces, we shape steel into work with a distinct architectural presence. Our catalog brings these pieces together, while custom requests start with the form, scale, finish and space you have in mind.

View Catalog

We create low-poly metal sculptures that feel both engineered and artistic. The work is designed to sit comfortably in residential interiors, outdoor landscapes, hotel lobbies, office entries and branded environments where a memorable focal point matters.

Sharp geometryLow-poly planes give every sculpture a clean silhouette and strong modern identity.
Metal permanenceSteel construction and durable finishes make the work suitable for long-term display.
Statement placementPieces are created for places where art must be seen, remembered and photographed.
Process

From sharp model to finished steel.

Every piece follows a direct production workflow so the geometry stays clean and the sculpture feels premium.

View Catalog

Model selection & sculpting

A low-poly animal, emblem or object is selected, refined and prepared as a sculptural 3D form.

Build drawings

The 3D form is translated into clean build drawings with panels, angles and assembly logic.

Laser cutting & forming

Steel sheets are laser-cut, then each piece is bent and shaped into its proper low-poly angle.

Welding & finish

The sculpture is welded, refined, cleaned and finished with the right coating for its space.

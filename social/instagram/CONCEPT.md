# Ironform Instagram — Studio Form / v1

Created 2026-10-29. Persistent visual direction requested by the user. First example: `panther-01`.

## Direction

Carry the website's restrained architectural character into Instagram: warm ivory plaster, pale limestone, directional daylight, charcoal typography, one strong metal sculpture. Quiet, confident, spacious. Use clean neutral sans-serif typography with light/regular weights. Avoid ornamental fonts, promotional badges, prices, busy grids, heavy borders, excessive slogans and unrelated decoration.

## Cover system

- Portrait 4:5, export 1080 × 1350, sRGB JPG; retain generated PNG masters.
- Top left: small widely spaced IRONFORM STUDIO wordmark text. Top right: 01 / actual slide count.
- Category in small spaced uppercase above the product title.
- Large two-line product name in the upper third; allow generous breathing room.
- One complete sculpture dominates the center/lower image. Keep its silhouette, pose, proportions, panel layout and construction faithful. No invented product features.
- Bottom: fine rule, concise finish message, tiny material swatches, ironformstudio.com. Keep text within a safe margin of roughly 5–8%.
- Palette: charcoal #171717, warm ivory #EEE8DC, stone #D6C7B3. Metal colors come from approved product images.
- Panter copy: TR “ZEMİN HEYKELLERİ”, “Zemin / Panteri”, “Beş yüzey. Tek form.”; EN “FLOOR SCULPTURES”, “Floor / Panther”, “Five finishes. One form.”
- For other products, adapt category, product name, number of finishes and slide count truthfully. Do not copy five finishes onto a four-finish product.

## Language and account rules

Produce TWO separate covers with the same design: Turkish for @ironform_studiotr, English for @ironform_studio (United States of America). Localize only the text when deriving the second cover. Do not combine both languages on one cover. Never describe the US account as international.

## Carousel system

Only the cover is newly edited/generated. Slide 2 is the existing clean, text-free AI photograph that the cover is based on. Then show existing finish variants separately, followed by one existing alternate angle. Reuse assets; do not regenerate every photograph. Exporting JPG and resizing proportionally for consistent upload dimensions is allowed, with no retouch, color change, overlays or new crops on the subsequent slides.

For panther v1: 7 slides: cover; clean matte black; chrome; gold; black mirror chrome; rose gold; alternate front angle. Source filenames are recorded in `panther-01/carousel.json`. “Clean original” in this workflow means the unedited existing AI asset, not real product photography. Captions disclose visualization where appropriate.

## Reproduction workflow

1. Select existing approved product hero and finish assets; inspect them.
2. Read the panther cover PNGs as visual references alongside this guide.
3. Create only the first cover with built-in imagegen, preserving the product and using the established hierarchy.
4. Inspect product fidelity, text spelling, safe margins and material count.
5. Derive the second language cover from the approved first cover with text-only localization.
6. Package both language carousels in numbered order, with captions and original source mappings. Save prompts and masters.
7. Do not publish to Instagram without the user's explicit instruction.

Reference deliverables: `panther-01/tr/01-kapak.jpg` and `panther-01/en/01-cover.jpg`. The covers are the visual reference; the numeric palette is guidance rather than a requirement to recolor existing images.
